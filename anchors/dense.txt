The canonical ingress path is a close-bounded, single-writer admission seam: the UI emits a work-request envelope into the durable intent ledger, which serves as the authoritative source of truth for desired-state materialization. A leader-fenced reconciler then performs level-triggered convergence over the bounded work set, hydrating each intent into a hermetic, owner-scoped execution unit. Status is surfaced via a monotonic, append-only projection, preserving the load-bearing invariant that observed state never outruns the durable frontier. This keeps the blast radius tightly scoped and the failure surface well-characterized, with a clean ownership boundary at the reconciliation seam.
